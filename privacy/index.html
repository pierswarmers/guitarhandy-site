GuitarHandy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

GuitarHandy is a guitar app for tuning, playing songs, learning and practising. It is built so that your information stays with you. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Microphone. GuitarHandy listens to your guitar to tune it, record loops and practice clips, check your timing and find your singing key. Sound is processed on your device as you play. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Loops. Loops you record stay on your device. They leave it only if you choose to share or export them yourself.
  • Your songs, progress and practice clips. Your songbook, setlists, settings, practice progress and the short practice clips you record are stored on your device, and in your own private iCloud if you use it (see below).

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, GuitarHandy keeps your songs, setlists, practice progress and practice clips in your private iCloud database, so they follow you to your other devices. This data belongs to your Apple Account and is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on this device only.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Apple Music

If you allow it, GuitarHandy uses Apple Music on your device to play a song along with its chart. Your library and listening stay with Apple; we receive nothing.

Importing a chord page

When you import a chord chart from a link, your device fetches that page directly from the website you chose, the same way a browser would. We are not involved in that request and keep no record of it. The website may log the request under its own privacy policy.

Children

GuitarHandy does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
