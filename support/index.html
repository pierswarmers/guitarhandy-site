GuitarHandy Support

GuitarHandy is a guitar app for tuning, playing songs, learning and practising. The tuner is free; one subscription unlocks everything else, with nothing else to buy.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device? Open GuitarHandy, tap any locked tool, then tap Restore. Or go to Songs › Settings › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or change my plan? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › GuitarHandy. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal.

Does my family get it too? Yes. GuitarHandy supports Family Sharing, so up to five family members can use the same subscription.

My songs aren't on my other device. Songs, setlists and practice progress sync through your private iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices, and that iCloud Drive is on. Settings in GuitarHandy shows whether iCloud is connected.

The tuner doesn't hear my guitar. GuitarHandy needs the microphone. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn GuitarHandy on, or tap Open Settings on the tuner. Sound is processed on your device and never leaves it.

Can I use a page-turn pedal? Yes. Bluetooth page-turn pedals step through songs and setlists on stage.
